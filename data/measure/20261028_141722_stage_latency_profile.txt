--- ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS & COMMIT BEFORE PHASE 1 COMPARISON ---
1. Cause of Constant ~2.07s Memory Latency:
   - Settings and adapters previously pointed to 'http://localhost:11434'.
   - On Windows, resolving 'localhost' attempts IPv6 (::1) first. Because Ollama listens on IPv4 only,
     each connection blocked for a 2.0-second socket timeout before falling back to IPv4 (127.0.0.1).
   - Fixing host to 'http://127.0.0.1:11434' reduced memory embedding latency from 2,070ms down to ~120-180ms (a 15x speedup).
2. Attachment Retrieval Gating:
   - Plain chats without attachments completely bypass attachment retrieval.
   - Conversations with attachments gate retrieval with is_query_potentially_document_related and
     min_similarity threshold (0.35), ensuring unrelated chatter does not inject document context.
