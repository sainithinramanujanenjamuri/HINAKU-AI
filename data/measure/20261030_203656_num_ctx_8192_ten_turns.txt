2. Design & Development: Build data pipelines, models, and supply chains. Risks include poisoning, bias, privacy issues, and IP theft.
3. Verification & Validation: Test functionality, performance, fairness, security, and human evaluation. Ask if it works for the intended users under specific conditions.

5. Operation & Monitoring: Watch for accuracy drift, bias gaps, latency, security attacks, and data drift. Follow the loop of Monitor → Alert → Investigate → Mitigate → Verify.
6. Re-evaluation: Retrain or recalibrate models to reassess fairness, privacy, and security before approving changes.
7. Retirement: Define end-of-life criteria, disable APIs, preserve audit evidence, and manage data retention/deletion safely.

The goal is not risk-free AI but systems whose risks are understood, governed, monitored, and justified.
