Backend: local | Model: bonsai-2:9b
Full Reply:
Topic 2 outlines seven iterative stages of building an AI system, emphasizing that deployment is not the end but a starting point for ongoing security and monitoring.

1.  **Inception/Scoping**: Define purpose, users, success criteria, and risk tolerance before writing code. Risk: Wrong problem framing or unclear ownership.
2.  **Design & Development**: Build data pipelines, models, and manage supply chain risks like poisoning or IP theft. Risk: Data bias or privacy leaks.
3.  **Verification & Validation**: Test functional security, performance, fairness, and human evaluation. Ask if it works for the intended users under specific conditions.
4.  **Deployment**: Implement controls for authentication, secure configuration, rollback mechanisms, and logging. Risk: Unauthorized access or unsafe automation integration.
5.  **Operation & Monitoring**: Continuously watch for accuracy drift, bias gaps, security attacks, and data drift. Follow a loop of Monitor → Alert → Investigate → Mitigate.
6.  **Re-evaluation**: Retrain models, reassess fairness, and verify changes before re-approving them.
7.  **Retirement**: Define end-of-life criteria, disable APIs, preserve audit evidence, and manage data deletion safely. Risk: Insecure systems left running or unmanaged data retention.
