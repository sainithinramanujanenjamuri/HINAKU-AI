Topic 2 outlines a seven-stage lifecycle where risks are managed continuously, not just at deployment.

**The Seven Stages:**
1. **Inception/Scoping:** Define purpose, users, and risk tolerance. Risk: wrong problem framing or unclear ownership.
2. **Design & Development:** Build data, models, and supply chains. Risks include poisoning, bias, and IP issues.
3. **Verification & Validation:** Test for function, fairness, and security. Ask if it works for whom under what conditions.
4. **Deployment:** Apply controls like authentication and secure secrets. Risk: unauthorized use or integration failure.
5. **Operation & Monitoring:** Watch for drift, bias gaps, and attacks. Use a loop of Monitor → Alert → Investigate → Mitigate → Verify.
6. **Re-evaluation:** Retrain models and reassess fairness/security before making changes.
7. **Retirement:** Define end-of-life criteria and decommission safely without just switching off the system.

**Core Principles:**
- Risk is prioritized using Likelihood × Impact (e.g., Data poisoning often scores Critical).
- Governance frameworks like NIST AI RMF or ISO/IEC 42001 apply throughout these stages.
- The goal is not risk-free AI, but understanding and governing risks.
